### Generated by us in a matching style

Drawn by our own Pillow script after ImprovedForever's `tools/make_ui_textures.py` (same disc,
rounded chip, ring and D-pad cross geometry and colours); the letters are rasterised from Arial
Bold. No ImprovedForever file is contained in them.

- `Art/Glyph_XB_A.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_B.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_X.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_Y.tga`
- `Art/Glyph_XB_LB.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_RB.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_LT.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_RT.tga`
- `Art/Glyph_XB_LS.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_RS.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_Start.tga`, `Art/Glyph_XB_Select.tga`
- `Art/Glyph_PS_Start.tga`, `Art/Glyph_PS_Select.tga`
- `Art/Glyph_DPad_Down.tga`, `Art/Glyph_DPad_Left.tga`, `Art/Glyph_DPad_Right.tga`,
  `Art/Glyph_DPad_UD.tga`

The hint-row layout in `Glyphs.lua` / `Legend.lua` (plate colours, 24 px glyph, 4 px gap, 10 px
padding, 15 px between hints) and the crossbar cooldown colours, 0.6 s ready flash, 1.6 s global
cooldown cut-off and set pips in `Bar.lua` follow the values of ImprovedForever's
`IF.InputLegend` (`ImprovedForever/Pad.lua`) and `ImprovedForever_Immersion/GamepadBar.lua`;
the Lua is our own, written for Lua 5.0 and the 1.12 API.

The radial wheel's wedges (`Art/Wheel_Fill.tga`, `Art/Wheel_Rim.tga`, `Art/Wheel_Glow.tga`) are
drawn by our own Pillow script after ImprovedForever's `tools/make_segments.py` (the signed-distance
slice, supersampled, and its rim bands), with the proportions ImprovedForever measured from
Forever's radial (`ImprovedForever_Wheel/Ring.lua`); no ImprovedForever file is contained in them.
The consumables wheel's "+N HP" / "+N Mana" values follow `ImprovedForever_Wheel/RingContent.lua`'s
parse of the item's "Use:" line, rewritten for Lua 5.0 and a 1.12 tooltip scan.

The vibration patterns in `Vibration.lua` take their step values from
`ImprovedForever_Vibration/Vibration.lua`'s pattern table, which ImprovedForever adapted from
Easy Controller - Forever (see the next section); the event handling is our own, for 1.12.
